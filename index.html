How it works

  1. 1

    A local group pins up what it needs

    They list the exact items and how many. Our team checks every group and notice before it goes up.

  2. 2

    You give what's still needed

    Pick an item, say how many, and see where to drop it off. Or lend a hand at an event.

  3. 3

    They receive it

    The group confirms your offer and marks it received, and the notice updates for everyone.
