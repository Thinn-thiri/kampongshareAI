How it works

  1. 1

    Organizations post what they need

    Local groups list exact items and quantities. An admin checks every organization and request first.

  2. 2

    You offer exactly what's needed

    Pick an item, say how many, and see where to drop it off. Or sign up to volunteer at an event.

  3. 3

    They confirm and receive

    The organization accepts your offer and marks it delivered — progress updates for everyone to see.
