Privacy Notice

Last updated 28 October 2026

KampongShare connects people in Singapore who want to give with organizations that need help. This notice explains, in plain words, what we collect, why, who can see it, and what you can do about it. We follow Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

KampongShare is a hackathon prototype. Please use made-up details when trying it out.

What we collect

  • Everyone: your name, email and password (the password is stored securely by our login provider; we can't see it).
  • Optional: phone number, postal code and area. We never ask for your full home address.
  • Volunteers (optional): the days and times you're free, your interests and skills.
  • What you do here: the donations you offer and the events you apply for.
  • Organizations: the organization's name, description, address and map location (these are public).
  • Your consent, the time you gave it, and that you confirmed you are 18 or older.
  • Messages you send to organizations, donors or volunteers on KampongShare.

We do not collect NRIC/FIN numbers, date of birth, race, religion, income, health information or photos of people.

Why we use it

  • To let you offer items and apply to volunteer, and let organizations arrange drop-offs and events with you.
  • To show needs and events near you (using your postal code or area).
  • To keep the platform safe: admins check organizations, requests and events before they go public.

We don't sell your data, and we don't use ads, trackers or analytics cookies.

Who can see it

  • The public sees organizations, their approved requests and events, and overall totals — never the names of donors or volunteers.
  • An organization sees your name and phone number only if you offered items to it or applied to its event. It never sees your offers to other organizations.
  • Admins see personal details only where needed to review and moderate.

Messages

  • Messages can only be read by the two people in the conversation. Our admins can read a message only if one of you reports it.
  • The other side sees your name. Please don't share bank details or passwords — donations are always free.
  • Either of you can block a conversation, so no new messages can be sent. You can report any message you receive.
  • Deleting a conversation removes it from your inbox only. The other person keeps their copy, and we keep messages for 12 months for safety before deleting them permanently. To have your messages deleted sooner, contact us.

How we use AI

  • AI helps explain how urgent a request is and suggests which needs match what you can give. Every AI result is labelled: AI-suggested
  • The urgency score comes from a clear formula (deadline, how much is still needed, urgency set by the organization). AI only writes the explanation.
  • We never send personal data to the AI — only things like item names, quantities, deadlines and area names. Phone numbers and emails are removed first.
  • AI never uses race, religion, nationality, health or income.
  • For volunteers, we use your availability, interests and skills for suggestions only if you tick the matching box. You can turn it off any time.
  • People always make the decisions. AI only sorts and suggests.
  • In the Ask KampongShare chat you can tap a suggested question or type your own. It only answers questions about KampongShare, uses public information only, and never shares anyone's personal information. Phone numbers, emails and similar details you type are removed before anything else happens, and chats aren't stored.
  • You can use KampongShare in English, Chinese or Malay. AI answers are written in the language you choose (typed chat questions in the language you type), and only the language choice is sent with them.
  • AI never reads your messages.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while your account is open. When you delete your account, we remove your profile and personal details. Records of past donations may be kept for the organization, but without your name or contact details.

We keep your donation and volunteering history for 12 months so you and the organizations can see what happened. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Your choices

  • See and correct everything we hold about you on your profile page.
  • Withdraw your consent on your profile page. You won't be able to donate or volunteer until you agree again.
  • Turn AI matching off on your profile page.
  • Delete your account on your profile page (organization accounts: please contact us).
  • Your language choice is saved only in your browser.

Keeping it safe

The site only works over a secure connection (HTTPS). Database rules make sure people can only see what they're allowed to. Secret keys are kept on the server, never in the website. If a serious data breach ever happened, we would tell the Personal Data Protection Commission and the people affected promptly, as the law requires.

Questions?

Contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@kampongshare.example.com. We'll reply within 5 working days.
